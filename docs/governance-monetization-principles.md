# Governance Monetization Principles

Status: strategy boundary for post-v1 public dev-preview

Skill-Seeking Agent's credible wedge is governed capability acquisition. The
local CLI should remain the proof surface: it shows when an agent lacks a
capability, what skill evidence exists, why human review is still required, and
which boundaries prevent silent authority expansion.

This is not a current hosted product, production service, marketplace launch,
true sandbox, or autonomous promotion system.

## Principle

Explore monetization around governance, evidence, approvals, auditability, and
policy control. Do not lead with a public skill marketplace.

Marketplace-first incentives would push toward more tools and more routing
surface before the review, eval, and no-mutation boundaries are easy for
operators to trust.

## Credibility Layer

The local CLI remains the credibility layer for:

- capability-gap detection;
- structured skill requests;
- temporary skill validation;
- candidate evidence;
- human decision points;
- release, lifecycle, diagnostic, and no-mutation proof.

Paid or hosted surfaces should only build on proof already visible in the local
CLI. They should not replace local evidence with opaque authority.

## Exploratory Future Surfaces

Future commercial or open-core surfaces may explore:

- team evidence dashboards;
- policy-controlled skill admission;
- enterprise audit trails;
- eval suite management;
- approval workflows;
- private skill registries;
- compliance proof packets;
- hosted review or control planes over local agents.

These are strategy directions, not current release claims.

## Current Non-Goals

The public dev-preview should not claim or prioritize:

- hosted production service behavior;
- production safety;
- public skill marketplace support;
- true sandboxing;
- autonomous promotion;
- durable generated-skill admission into `skills/`;
- positive stable routing;
- permission widening without explicit review.
